Chalk & Aegean, across the range

One screen can't settle a palette. Below it runs across the app's three emotional registers: the commercial screen, the layered one, and the confirmation — where a cool ground is most at risk of feeling clinical.

4a is 2c exactly as you saw it. 4b is the same palette with the one change I'd argue for: terracotta comes back with a specific job — money and the primary action — while Aegean keeps structure and navigation. That's what stops chalk reading as generic, and it's a two-token difference.
